A physiotherapy organisation built around one idea: your quality of life.

Sunshine Physiotherapy provides personalized physiotherapy and rehabilitation care across Zimbabwe, currently through clinics in Victoria Falls and Hwange. Our clinical teams work with patients recovering from pain, injury, surgery, and mobility loss — combining hands-on assessment with structured, evidence-informed treatment plans.

Mission

To help every patient regain mobility, reduce pain, and return to the activities that matter to them, through personalized, evidence-informed physiotherapy.

Vision

To be a trusted physiotherapy organisation across Zimbabwe, known for accessible, high-quality, patient-centred rehabilitation care.
